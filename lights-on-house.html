Why it works

Each room teaches one thing you'll need when the power, water or roads fail: what food to keep, how to make water safe, where a generator goes, how to stay warm, and what to grab first. It takes about 10 minutes, and every answer comes from FEMA, the CDC, USDA, EPA or the CPSC.

72 HOURSTO 6 MONTHS

Liked the game? 72 Hours to 6 Months is the full plan behind it: power, water, food, medicine, money and family, from the first hour to the first six months. Start this weekend with about $100.
